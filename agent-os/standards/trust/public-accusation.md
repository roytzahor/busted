# Naming A Business In Public Is A Higher Bar Than Telling One User

`/store/[domain]` ("Is {domain} legit?") is indexed, permanent, names a real
business, and is generated by our AI rather than supplied by a third party.
Treat every word on it as a factual assertion we must be able to defend.

## The aggregation gate

A `dropship` verdict counts toward a public store verdict **only if its own
`presenceTier` is not `silent`** (`countsAsDropship()` in `lib/store/report.ts`).

A verdict at 0.35 confidence is one we would not say a word about to the user
who scanned that product. Counting it toward a public accusation would say
something louder, about a third party, on weaker evidence.

**The gate is deliberately asymmetric.** It applies to `dropship` (an
accusation) and never to `legit` (an exoneration, and `silent` by definition).
Gating both would empty the denominator and flag every store. Do not
"fix" this into symmetry.

A verdict that fails the gate counts as **neither** dropship nor legit. It is
an absence of evidence, not evidence of absence.

## Thresholds

- `MIN_DECISIVE_SCANS = 4`. Two scans is an anecdote, not a pattern.
- `FLAGGED_SHARE = 0.6`, `CLEAN_SHARE = 0.2`.
- `computeDomainTier()` (the extension's domain fallback) uses the same gate
  and is capped at `amber` — it is inferred from other products on the store,
  never a verdict on the page in front of the user.

## Presentation rules

- **No raw enums.** `insufficient_evidence` is an internal contract, not
  consumer copy. Map through `verdictLabel()`.
- **`flagged` uses `--primary`, never `--destructive`.** Red is the error
  colour everywhere else; a flagged store is a finding, not a fault.
- **The fire gradient is the flame register.** Do not put it above a headline
  that sits over "Not enough data yet".
- **Methodology goes above the evidence**, at readable size. A disclaimer at
  `text-xs` and 60% opacity below the fold is not a disclosure.

## Still open

There is **no merchant correction channel** anywhere in the product. A
right-of-reply is a defamation mitigant in most jurisdictions, and it is not
merchant money, so it does not touch the anti-strategy. Do not ship a
`mailto:` that routes nowhere — that is worse than none.

Jurisdiction matters: Israeli defamation law permits statutory damages without
proof of harm and puts the burden of proving truth on the defendant. A
US-centric §230 model does not apply here.
